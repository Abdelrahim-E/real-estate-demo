Privacy and cookies

Julian Vance is a fictional agent and this is a demo project. The listings, reviews and numbers are made up. This page shows how a small business site explains its data practices. A real agent needs a policy written for their own business and country.

What the site collects

Nothing. The contact form and the newsletter form are demonstrations: what you type is checked in your browser and is not sent, saved or shared.

Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies of its own. It remembers your light or dark choice in your browser's local storage, so the page looks the same next time. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking. The stock photos are loaded from Unsplash's servers, and those servers may set their own cookies. A real site would host its own photos.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts and photos load from Unsplash. Each service has its own privacy policy.

Refunds

The site sells nothing, so it has no refund policy. A real agent who takes payments or fees would need one.

Photos

Stock photos come from Unsplash and are used under its license.

Not legal advice

This is a template for a demo. It is not legal advice.
